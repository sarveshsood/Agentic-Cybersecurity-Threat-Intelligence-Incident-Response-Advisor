# Responsible AI Statement

ACTIRA uses AI to **assist** security operators, not to replace accountability.

We commit to:

1. **Human oversight** for critical outcomes
2. **Transparency** via citations and audit logs where designed
3. **Security & privacy** controls for secrets and log data
4. **Evaluability** via golden datasets and CI
5. **Honest limitations** — heuristic ATT&CK, mock TI without keys, no multi-tenant isolation yet

We will not market ACTIRA as autonomous cyber defense without human control.
